{\businglespacing\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{12pt}\itshape
Citations may be in alphabetical order by the surname of the main author,
or they may be numbered and arranged in order of appearance in the text. A
variation on the second style is that some students arrange all cites
alphabetically by main author, and then number them when the section is
complete. In this case, the cite numbers in the main text are not
sequential (e.g., 1, 2, 3) but will jump around (e.g., 34, 85, 12, 27)

If you have a references section at the end of each chapter, you must
have a cumulative bibliography at the end of the thesis, after any
appendices, and preceding your Curriculum Vitae.\par}}
